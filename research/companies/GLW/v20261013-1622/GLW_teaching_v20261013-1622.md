## ③ 术语 Glossary
- DCF (Discounted Cash Flow｜现金流折现): 把未来每年能拿到的现金，按"时间成本"折回今天加总。
- FCF (Free Cash Flow｜自由现金流) = 经营现金流 − 资本开支。
- WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital｜加权平均资本成本): 投资人要求的年回报率，用作折现率。
- Reverse DCF (反向DCF): 反过来问"现价要求公司增长多快"。
- Consensus (一致预期): 分析师预测的平均值。
- Falsifier (证伪指标): 一旦出现就说明论点错了的信号。

## ④ 生活比喻
买这家公司股票≈买一棵果树。DCF就是估算果树未来每年结多少果(FCF)，再考虑"明年的一斤果不如今天的一斤果值钱"(WACC折现)。

## ⑤ 因果箭头
Optical fiber / specialty glass 光纤/特种玻璃 需求 → 收入增长 (g) → 自由现金流 (FCF = 收入 × FCF率) → 折现 (÷(1+WACC)^t) → 每股价值 → 与股价比较 → 预期差

## ⑥ 数字例题（符号定义）
设 R₀ = 过去12个月收入 = 17.0 bn USD；g₁ = 第一年增速 = 19.6%；m = 长期FCF率 = 15.0%；W = WACC = 10.8%。
第一年收入 R₁ = R₀ × (1+g₁) = 20.3 bn USD。
若第5年FCF率达到 m，则第1年FCF ≈ R₁ × m₁（m₁ 由当前 14.1% 向 15.0% 线性过渡）= 2.9 bn USD；
折现到今天: FCF₁ ÷ (1+W)¹ = 2.6 bn USD。十年加总 + 终值 = EV 64.0 bn USD。

## ⑦ 事实 / 预测 / 市场预期
- 事实: 过去财务数据(第4节)、现价。
- 预测(我们的假设): 长期FCF率 15.0%、三情景。
- 市场预期: 一致预期增速 19.6%；价格隐含增速 39.7%。

## ⑧ 三情景
Bear $27 / Base $66 / Bull $113；概率加权 $68。

## ⑨ 反方观点与失效条件
反方: Display weakness, fiber pricing from Chinese competition, capex digestion｜显示疲弱、中国光纤价格竞争、Capex消化

# GLW Beginner Teaching Edition (English) v20261013-1622
## ① In one line
Corning Incorporated is a Optical fiber / specialty glass 光纤/特种玻璃 company. Price $147.28; our Base model value ≈ $66 (-55.0%).
## ② What happened
Last quarter EPS actual vs consensus: ['2026-06-30', '0.78', '0.75378', '0.0348']. Next earnings ≈ 2026-10-27 (TENTATIVE).
## ③ Glossary
DCF (Discounted Cash Flow｜现金流折现); FCF (Free Cash Flow｜自由现金流) = operating cash flow − capex; WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital｜加权平均资本成本) = the yearly return investors demand; Reverse DCF (反向DCF) = what growth the price requires; Consensus (一致预期); Falsifier (证伪指标).
## ④ Analogy
Owning the stock ≈ owning a fruit tree: DCF estimates future fruit (FCF) and discounts it because fruit next year is worth less than fruit today.
## ⑤ Causal arrows
Demand → revenue growth (g) → FCF (= revenue × FCF margin) → discounting ÷(1+WACC)^t → value/share → compare with price → expectation gap
## ⑥ Worked example (symbols)
R₀ = TTM revenue = 17.0 bn USD; g₁ = 19.6%; m = long-run FCF margin = 15.0%; W = 10.8%.
R₁ = R₀×(1+g₁) = 20.3 bn USD; FCF₁ = 2.9 bn USD; PV = FCF₁/(1+W) = 2.6 bn USD; 10 years + terminal → EV 64.0 bn USD.
## ⑦ Fact vs forecast vs market expectation
Facts: reported financials, price. Forecast: our margin/scenario assumptions. Market: consensus growth 19.6%; price-implied growth 39.7%.
## ⑧ Three scenarios
Bear $27 / Base $66 / Bull $113; weighted $68.
## ⑨ Counterview & failure conditions
Display weakness, fiber pricing from Chinese competition, capex digestion｜显示疲弱、中国光纤价格竞争、Capex消化 — Falsifiers: Optical Comms growth decelerates below enterprise market; Springboard margin targets missed｜光通信增速回落；Springboard利润率目标落空
