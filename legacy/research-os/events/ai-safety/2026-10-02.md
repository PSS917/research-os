## ARCHIVE CLUSTER A — WH Joint Commitment + SI naming EO (2026-09-29, post prior MYT AM)

### Facts｜事实

**EN**
1. **CONFIRMED FACT (Washington Examiner full text + CNBC 09-30 context):** After a White House meeting with frontier-lab / tech CEOs, participants signed the **White House Accord on Super Intelligence — Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities**. Signers reported: Anthropic (Amodei), OpenAI (Brockman), Google (Pichai), Meta (Zuckerberg), SpaceXAI (Musk), Nvidia (Huang).
2. **CONFIRMED FACT (accord full text):** Four voluntary layers — (1) robust internal controls on cyber / bio / chem threats and unintended hacking during training/deployment; (2) empowered internal oversight team; (3) independent external auditor/evaluator; (4) independent board committee receiving reports and ensuring remediation. Companies “will meet regularly” on standards; text says codification into law/regulation “may make sense” later — **nonbinding**.
3. **CONFIRMED FACT (WH Fact Sheet 2026-09-29):** EO directs executive branch to use “Super Intelligence” / “SI” (not “AI”) in non-statutory docs; APST to propose a federal SI definition. Primary: https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-inaugurates-the-era-of-super-intelligence/
4. **CONFIRMED ABSENCE:** No FLOPs threshold, licensing regime, export-control instrument, or CapEx mandate in the Accord or SI-naming Fact Sheet.

**简体中文**
1. **确认事实：** 白宫会后签署《超级智能白宫协定——前沿责任联合承诺》；报道签字人含 Amodei、Brockman、Pichai、Zuckerberg、Musk、Huang（英伟达）。
2. **确认事实：** 四层自愿控制——内部监控（网络/生物/化学与非预期入侵）、内部监督团队、独立外部审计/评估、独立董事委员会；可定期会商标准；文本称日后“或许”成法——**无约束力**。

4. **确认缺失：** 协定与命名事实清单中**无**算力阈值、许可制、出口管制工具或 CapEx 强制要求。
