## MATERIAL ALERT 1 — Frontier “pacing” cluster (12–17 Sep 2026)

### Facts｜事实

**EN**
1. **CONFIRMED FACT:** On ~2026-09-12, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published *We Must Pace the Frontier*, arguing labs must **slow the rate of capability improvement** (not halt training) so alignment/safety can keep up. Drivers cited: recursive self-improvement (RSI) since ~summer 2026; OpenAI–Hugging Face agent swarm incident. Three-step plan: (1) **embedded third-party evaluators** (Anthropic unilateral commitment); (2) **democratic coordination** on standards and limits (may need government mediation / narrow antitrust waiver); (3) **global coordination** with China under verification constraints. Primary: https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier
2. **CONFIRMED FACT (Reuters 2026-09-12):** Sam Altman said OpenAI will also commit to independent evaluators with employee-like access and will share more “soon”; Elon Musk also expressed agreement. https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-ceo-urges-ai-companies-slow-model-development-2026-09-12/
3. **CONFIRMED FACT (CNBC 2026-09-15):** OpenAI spokesperson confirmed ongoing talks with Anthropic and Google on collaborative safety / standards-body ideas since Demis Hassabis’s July FINRA-like proposal. No signed org/charter. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/open-ai-google-anthropic-safety.html
4. **CONFIRMED FACT (essay):** Amodei’s democratic-pacing section explicitly urges: **do not sell powerful AI chips or semiconductor manufacturing equipment to China**; crack down on smuggling and **remote access** to overseas DCs; crack down on unauthorized distillation; strengthen weight security — framed as preserving US lead so pacing is feasible.
5. **CONFIRMED FACT (MLex 2026-09-15):** FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson said combining regulation requests with an **antitrust exemption** “sure sounds like moat-digging”; deeply suspicious of incumbents seeking both.
6. **CONFIRMED FACT (Reuters 2026-09-17):** DOJ Antitrust AAG Stanley Woodward said coordinating on cybersecurity/security “doesn’t… appear” anticompetitive; Division open to meetings; **frontier labs have not requested** an antitrust meeting; reviewing whether to update existing cybersecurity coordination guidance. OpenAI’s Chris Lehane said talks with rivals do **not** need a waiver (Bloomberg via Reuters).

**简体中文**
1. **确认事实：** 约2026-09-12 Anthropic CEO Amodei发表《We Must Pace the Frontier》，主张**放慢能力提升速度**（非停训），以便对齐/安全跟上；三点：嵌入式第三方评估、民主国家协调、全球协调。
2. **确认事实：** Altman表态OpenAI也将引入类员工权限的独立评估者，并将“很快”公布细节。
