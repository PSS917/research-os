## 8. Thinking logic & transmission | 思考逻辑与传导

### English

1. **Price the binary first.** With FedWatch ~90%+, the hike itself should not move risk assets much unless vote/language/SEP surprise. That is what happened on equities: modest green.
2. **Judge the path second.** Median dots say: one more hike in 2026, then **stay at 4.1% through 2027**. That is restrictive for discount rates even if it is **not** a 100 bp 2026–27 hike avalanche.
3. **Read framing third.** Unanimous vote + dropped supply-shock attribution raise the odds the Committee treats inflation as **too broad to ignore**, including oil/tariffs/AI CapEx demand channels cited in wires (RELIABLE context, not Fed admitting “anti-AI”).
4. **Transmission to coverage:** `Path/real yields → WACC → AI CapEx financing & multiples → accelerator/memory/optical timing`. Decision-day relief ≠ multiple expansion if 10Y real stays ~2.6% and funds stay ~4.1% into 2027.
5. **Do not confuse Warsh silence with dovishness.** No statement guidance was expected; the SEP did the talking. Press still incomplete.

### 简体中文

1. **先看二元决议。** 加息已被高概率定价，股票小涨符合“兑现交易”。  
2. **再看路径。** 中位数：2026年再加一次，2027年维持4.1%——对贴现率仍紧，但不是极鹰的连加叙事。  
3. **再看框架。** 全票 + 删除供给冲击表述 → 更不愿“看透”油价。  
4. **传导到覆盖股：** 决议日舒缓 ≠ 估值扩张；关键利率与4.1%路径仍压长久期。  
5. **沃什少说 ≠ 鸽派；** SEP已说话，记者会待补全。
