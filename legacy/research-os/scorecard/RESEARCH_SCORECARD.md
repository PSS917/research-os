## Metric definitions | 指标定义

| Metric | What it measures | Hit / miss idea |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Forecast accuracy** | Direction/magnitude of fundamental outcomes vs pre-event forecast | Outcome within stated band / sign |
| **Scenario discipline** | Did realized path land in a pre-registered scenario with coherent assumptions? | Assigned scenario vs actual |
| **Probability calibration** | Stated probs vs frequency of outcomes | Calibration curve over n events |
| **Expectation-gap quality** | Was the gap (vs priced expectations) correctly signed/sized? | Gap call vs subsequent repricing |
| **Market-reaction call** | Predicted reaction vs observed (given what was priced) | Reaction sign/elasticity band |
| **Timing** | Catalyst window / when gap closes | Inside vs outside stated window |
| **Elasticity** | Abnormal return / \|surprise score\| vs prior category norms | Within historical band or flagged regime shift |
| **Valuation discipline** | Method fit, no false precision, fund. vs market-implied separation | Process checklist pass/fail |
| **Falsification discipline** | Pre-registered falsifiers checked; thesis killed or updated when hit | Falsifier honored vs ignored |

Error classes (for Notes / Biases): Fundamental | Expectation-Pricing | Transmission | Market-Reaction | Timing | Probability Calibration
