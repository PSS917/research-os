Thesis 投资论点

CoreWeave以高杠杆换取AI算力租赁的高速增长；核心是积压订单(backlog)转化率、融资成本与GPU经济寿命。FCF深度为负属于商业模式特征。

CoreWeave trades high leverage for fast AI-compute rental growth; the crux is backlog conversion, financing cost and GPU economic life. Deeply negative FCF is a feature of the model.

Falsifiers 证伪条件

Active power MW below plan; interest expense outruns EBITDA growth; H100/B200 rental prices fall sharply｜在运营电力低于计划；利息增速超EBITDA；GPU租价暴跌

Bull / Bear

▲ Backlog converts on time; financing spreads tighten; contract diversification｜积压订单按期转化；融资利差收窄；客户分散

▼ Delays in data-center delivery, refinancing stress, GPU price deflation｜交付延期、再融资压力、GPU租赁价格通缩
