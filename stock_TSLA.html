Thesis 投资论点

汽车主业利润率受价格战压缩，估值主要来自Robotaxi/FSD与Optimus期权以及储能高增长；股价对自动驾驶里程碑的叙事极度敏感(反身性强)。

Core auto margins are compressed by price competition; valuation mostly rests on Robotaxi/FSD and Optimus optionality plus fast-growing storage. The stock is highly reflexive to autonomy milestones.

Falsifiers 证伪条件

Robotaxi fleet/cities not expanding by guided dates; auto GM ex-credits declining; storage deployments flat｜Robotaxi未按期扩张；扣除碳积分汽车毛利下降；储能装机持平

Bull / Bear

▲ Unsupervised FSD scales across cities; Robotaxi unit economics proven; storage margins stay high｜无监督FSD多城扩张；Robotaxi单位经济验证；储能高毛利

▼ Auto volumes/margins keep falling; robotaxi incidents/regulation; key-person risk｜销量与毛利下滑；Robotaxi事故/监管；关键人物风险
