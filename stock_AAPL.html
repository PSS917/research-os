Thesis 投资论点

苹果是高质量现金流+回购的复利机器；AI(Apple Intelligence)换机周期是主要上行变量，存储成本上涨与服务业务监管是主要压力。

Apple is a high-quality cash-flow and buyback compounder; an AI-driven upgrade cycle is the main upside variable, while memory-cost inflation and services regulation are the main pressures.

Falsifiers 证伪条件

iPhone units decline y/y in upgrade cycle; services growth <10%; GM guide down｜换机年iPhone仍同比下滑；服务增速<10%；毛利指引下调

Bull / Bear

▲ AI upgrade supercycle; services growth sustains; margin holds despite memory costs｜AI换机潮；服务持续增长；毛利率扛住存储成本

▼ Memory cost squeezes GM; Google TAC payment ruling; China share loss｜存储成本压毛利；谷歌分成裁决；中国份额流失
