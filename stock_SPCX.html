Thesis 投资论点

上市后的SpaceX核心价值在Starlink订阅现金流与发射垄断地位；估值高度依赖Starship降本与直连手机(D2C)等远期期权，市场定价包含大量远期增长。

Post-IPO SpaceX value rests on Starlink subscription cash flow plus launch dominance; valuation leans heavily on long-dated options (Starship cost-down, direct-to-cell), so the price embeds aggressive long-run growth.

Falsifiers 证伪条件

Starlink subscriber growth decelerating for 2+ quarters; Starship flight-rate targets missed; FCF stays negative｜Starlink用户增速连续两季放缓；星舰发射次数不达标；FCF持续为负

Bull / Bear

▲ Starship reusability drives cost/kg sharply lower; D2C and enterprise/airline adoption accelerate｜星舰复用大幅降本；D2C与航空/企业加速

▼ IPO-lockup supply, Starship delays, regulatory/spectrum pushback, governance concentration｜解禁抛压、星舰延误、监管/频谱、治理集中
